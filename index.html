The property

A quiet garden in the middle of everything.

RElaxed City Living sits on 12th Avenue in Walmer — a leafy, walkable suburb that happens to be minutes from almost everywhere you would want to be in Gqeberha.

Each unit is self-catering with its own entrance and its own parking, so you come and go on your own schedule. Between them is a shared pool, a covered braai area and a garden with room to sit outside — the part guests tend to write home about.

  • Six independent units, from a studio to a two-bedroom apartment
  • Pool, covered braai and garden shared between the units
  • Solar, inverters and water tanks — the lights and WiFi stay on
  • Secure off-street parking at every unit

The area

Walmer puts you five minutes from most things.

You are next door to Bay Eye Hospital, a short walk from Walmer Park Shopping Centre and the Main Road restaurant strip, and a few minutes’ drive from the airport.

  • 5 min — Chief Dawid Stuurman International Airport
  • 10 min — Hobie Beach, Kings Beach and the Boardwalk
  • Walking distance — Walmer Park, Main Road restaurants and coffee shops
  • 15 min — Baakens Valley trails and the Nelson Mandela Bay Stadium
  • 45 min — Addo Elephant National Park gate
