Copper Dreams

Exposed brick, copper pipework and a proper bath. The most characterful unit on the property, and the best value.

Home / Rooms / Copper Dreams

About the unit

Copper Dreams has a look of its own — exposed brick, raw copper pipework running across the walls, patinated finishes and salvaged pieces. If you like a room with some personality rather than another beige box, this is the one.

It sleeps two adults and a child on a double bed with a sleeper couch. The en-suite bathroom has a bath as well as a shower. A JBL sound bar and uncapped Netflix cover the evenings, and there is a bar fridge, microwave, toaster and kettle for the basics.
