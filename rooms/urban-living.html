About the unit

Urban Living is a full one-bedroom apartment rather than a studio — a private bedroom with a king bed, a separate lounge with a double sleeper couch, a full kitchen and a modern bathroom. It is the unit to pick if you want your own space to spread out in.

The patio is the best part: private, and set up for a coffee in the morning or a drink at the end of the day. It suits couples, two friends travelling together, a small family, or anyone in town for work who would rather not live out of a hotel room.
