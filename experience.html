Nearby

The full experience.

Walmer puts you within easy reach of the beachfront, the Garden Route and some of the Eastern Cape’s best game reserves. Here is what we send guests to when they ask what to do.

Home / Experience

Ocean adventures

  • ProDive — scuba diving, PADI courses and boat dives
  • Nearby beaches: Hobie Beach, King’s Beach, Sardinia Bay, Maitlands and Schoenmakerskop

Trails

  • Fattracks — mountain bike trails through the Baakens Valley
  • Crossways — over 200km of hiking and biking trails on the farm
  • Norm Hudlin Trails — purpose-built single-track through coastal forest

Day trips & excursions

  • Sundaze — Sundays River cruises, sandboarding and tubing
  • Tsitsikamma — forest canopy tours, ziplines and the Storms River mouth
  • Jbay — world-class surf and a laid-back beach town
  • St Francis — canals, golf and a quieter stretch of coast

Game reserves

  • Schotia — day and night safaris on the Eastern Cape’s oldest private reserve
  • Addo — elephant, lion and the Big 7, about 30 minutes away
  • Shamwari — luxury Big Five safari lodges
